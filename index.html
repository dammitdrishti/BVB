Kulapati Kanhaiyalal Maneklal Munshi

Kulapati Kanhaiyalal Maneklal Munshi founded the Bhavan on Monday, November 7, 1938. It was the beginning of an 'Adventure in Faith'. A faith in India's Past, Present and Future.


The Bhavan steadfastly adheres to the Vedic ideal: "Let noble thoughts come to us from every side." It seeks to inculcate a value-based life, promoting ethical and spiritual values in everything it does.
