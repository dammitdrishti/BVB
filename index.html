Kulapati Kanhaiyalal Maneklal Munshi

Kulapati Kanhaiyalal Maneklal Munshi founded the Bhavan on November 7, 1938. It was the beginning of an 'Adventure in Faith'—faith in India's Past, Present and Future[cite: 1, 3].

The founding was based on the preservation and propagation of Bharatiya Sanskriti, the mother of languages, the Akshaya Patra, the inexhaustible reservoir[cite: 1].
